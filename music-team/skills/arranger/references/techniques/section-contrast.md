# Technique: Section Contrast

Contrast is what a listener hears at a boundary: something changes. Without it, even a high energy
number reads as the same section repeated. Decide the change at each boundary before choosing
instruments.

## Boundary moves

| Boundary | Move | Example row content |
|----------|------|---------------------|
| Verse to pre-chorus | Add rhythm: drums enter, bass starts to walk | `instruments_in`: kick, snare |
| Pre-chorus to chorus | Subtract for one beat or bar, then add everything on the downbeat | Last bar: only vocal and kick |
| Chorus to verse | Remove the top layer and the doubles; keep one carry-over | `instruments_out`: pad, vocal doubles |
| Verse 1 to verse 2 | Same bones, one new element; the listener hears progress | `instruments_in`: shaker |
| Into the bridge | Strip to two or three layers, change timbre or feel | half-time, piano and voice |
| Bridge to final chorus | Fill, pause, or a rise in pitch, then the biggest section | drum break, then all layers |
| Last chorus to outro | Remove layers in reverse order of entry | thinning to the opening sound |

## Entries and exits

- Enter on a downbeat of a phrase; exit on the last bar of a phrase.
- One entrance per 2 bars is plenty; stacked entrances at the same bar read as a single event.
- A layer that leaves must have a reason: space for the vocal, a drop before the chorus, or a reset.
- Bring back the first verse's instrument at the outro so the song closes on its opening color.

## Subtraction before a peak

The strongest chorus entrance follows the emptiest bar before it. Plan a gap of one beat to one bar:
cut drums, cut bass, or hold a single sustained layer, then restore every layer together. Do this at
the first chorus lightly, and at the final chorus fully.

## Repeats

Repeated labels (Chorus 2, Chorus 3) must not be identical rows. Each repeat adds one thing: a harmony,
a counter-line, an octave lift, a rhythmic variation, or a bigger fill. If nothing can be added, raise
the energy by one point through width alone and say so in `texture_note`.

## Checks

- Every boundary has at least one entry in `instruments_in` or `instruments_out`, or a stated texture change.
- No two adjacent rows have identical energy and identical instrument lists.
- The bridge differs from the verses in at least two of: layer set, rhythmic feel, register, timbre.
