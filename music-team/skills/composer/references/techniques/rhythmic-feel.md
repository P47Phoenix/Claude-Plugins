# Rhythmic Feel

Feel is where the notes sit against the pulse: straight or swung, on the beat or behind it, dense or sparse.

## Subdivision

| Feel | Subdivision | Written in ABC (`L:1/8`, 4/4) | Genres |
|------|-------------|-------------------------------|--------|
| Straight eighths | two per beat, equal | `A B A B` | pop, rock, country, folk |
| Straight sixteenths | four per beat | `L:1/16` or `A/ B/` | hip-hop, electronic |
| Swing | long-short, about 2:1 | write straight eighths and mark `Q`; swing is a performance feel | jazz, blues |
| Shuffle | triplet-based long-short | `(3A B A` or `6/8` | blues, country |
| Compound (6/8, 12/8) | groups of three | `M:6/8`, `L:1/8` | ballad, gospel |

ABC cannot notate swing; keep the written rhythm straight and state the feel in the structure notes or the arrangement.

## Syncopation

Accent weak beats or the off-beat: an eighth on the "and" of 2 tied across the beat, or an anticipated chord on the "and" of 4. It gives drive. Use it in 1-2 bars of a phrase, not all; resolve with a plain downbeat.

## Placing syllables

| Word type | Rhythm placement |
|-----------|------------------|
| Stressed syllable | on a downbeat or a longer note |
| Unstressed syllable | on an off-beat or a shorter note |
| Final word of a line | longest note of the line |
| Pickup syllables (a, the, I) | before the bar line, shorter notes |

Count the syllables per bar: a 4/4 bar with `L:1/8` holds 8 eighths, so a line of 8 syllables in two bars is eighths and quarters mixed; a line of 5 in two bars leaves room for rests and long notes.

## Pickup and downbeat

A pickup (anacrusis) is a short first bar that supplies the unstressed syllables before the first downbeat. The gate tolerates a short first bar and a short final bar. Pair the pickup with the final bar so total beats are consistent.

## Rests and space

Rest on beats 1 or 3 to give the voice a breath at phrase ends. Rests take no syllable. A bar of rest between phrases is fine; in `w:` it is an empty segment.

## Groove by genre (use the profile's `## Rhythm` for specifics)

| Genre family | Feel | Typical melody rhythm |
|--------------|------|------------------------|
| pop, rock | straight eighths, backbeat 2 and 4 | quarters and eighths, some syncopation in the chorus |
| country, folk | straight or lilting 6/8 | long vowels on quarter notes |
| blues, jazz | swung or shuffled | behind the beat, triplet figures |
| hip-hop | sixteenth grid | dense, rhythmic, small pitch range |
| gospel, soul | rolling compound or slow straight | melisma on long notes |
| electronic | straight, grid-locked | repeated short figures |

## Melisma

Spread one syllable over several notes with `_` in `w:` (it extends the previous syllable over the next note). Count each `_` as one slot. Reserve melisma for the hook's last word and for genres that expect it.

## Checklist

| Check | Pass when |
|-------|-----------|
| Bar sums | every bar equals the meter |
| Strong beats | stressed syllables land there |
| Variety | not every bar has the same rhythm |
| Breath | at least a short rest or long note at every phrase end |
| Tempo | `Q:1/4=<bpm>` equals `structure.md` |
