# Genre profile: Country

## Identity
Narrative songwriting with concrete detail, clean twangy instrumentation, and a conversational vocal. A small story, a clever title turn, and a chorus that lands the point.
Keep when adapting INTO country: the story, the twist on the title line, and steel, fiddle, or acoustic guitar colour.

## Lyric
- Diction: conversational, plain-spoken, with regional idiom and a touch of humour.
- POV: first-person storyteller; the narrator is an ordinary person.
- Lines: 7-10 syllables; four stresses; tight rhymes and full lines.
- Rhyme: ABCB or ABAB; perfect rhymes preferred; title set up by the verse and paid off in the chorus.
- Imagery: trucks, back roads, porches, kitchens, bars, church, rivers, small towns, dirt.
- Form: VCVCBC; verse-refrain for older styles; the bridge pivots to a twist.
- Hook: a title with a double meaning, repeated at the end of the chorus.
- Topics: love and heartbreak, home, family, work, faith, drinking, summer nights.
- Verses add one concrete detail each; the chorus states the idea in one clear line.

## Harmony
- Major: I - IV - V; I - V - vi - IV; I - IV - I - V; I - I7 - IV - iv.
- Minor: i - bVII - bVI - V; i - iv - bVII - bIII.
- Mostly major triads with added 6th and 7th; sus4 and walk-ups on the bass.
- Cadences: strong V-I, with the IV-I plagal "amen" tag.
- Keys: G, D, A, E, C; modulating up a whole step for the last chorus is common.
- Walk-ups and walk-downs between chords with passing bass notes.
- Occasional secondary dominants, such as II7 to V.

## Rhythm
- Tempo: 70–140 BPM; ballads at 60–80 BPM, two-steps at 100–130 BPM.
- Meter: 4/4; 3/4 waltz and 6/8 ballads.
- Feel: straight with a light shuffle in two-steps.
- Groove: bass on 1 and 3 (root and fifth), snare on 2 and 4 or train-beat brushed snare, acoustic strum in steady eighths.
- Phrases: 4-bar units; 8-bar verses and 8-bar choruses.
- Stops and a bright kick-snare push into the chorus.

## Arrangement
- Core: acoustic guitar, electric guitar, bass, drums; pedal steel, fiddle, piano, banjo, mandolin as colour.
- Texture: warm and open; leads trade fills in the vocal gaps.
- Arc: short intro hook, verse, chorus, verse, chorus, instrumental break, bridge, final chorus with a key lift.
- Production: clear vocal on top, bright acoustic guitars, tight bass, gentle compression, natural reverb.
- Vocals: clear, conversational, a touch of twang; harmony on the chorus.

## Adaptation notes
- Convert to 4/4 straight at 90-125 BPM with a two-step groove; replace synth pads with steel, fiddle, or acoustic guitar.
- Reharmonize to I-IV-V with walk-ups and add the plagal tag; reduce extended chords to 6ths and 7ths.
- Re-fit lyrics: craft a story with concrete details, move the twist to the chorus, tighten rhymes to perfect rhymes.
- Pitfall: generic emotion with no specific object or place.
- Pitfall: too much polish, which hides the instruments.
- Pitfall: losing the title pay-off in the last chorus.
