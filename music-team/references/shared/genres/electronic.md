# Genre profile: Electronic

## Identity
Synthesized and sequenced music built for the dance floor: pulsing four-on-the-floor kick, evolving textures, and energy shaped by build and drop.
Keep when adapting INTO electronic: the steady pulse, the build-and-drop arc, and synth-based hooks.

## Lyric
- Diction: minimal, repetitive, chantlike; short imperative or emotive phrases.
- POV: first-person or collective "we"; often a sung vocal topline over instrumental sections.
- Lines: 3-6 syllables; short slogans repeated and varied with filters.
- Rhyme: not required; repetition does the work; occasional AABB couplets.
- Imagery: light, night, movement, heartbeat, fire, sky, freedom, escape.
- Form: edm-build-drop is the template; VCVCBC with a drop replacing the chorus also works.
- Hook: a vocal chop or synth lead that plays in the drop; the title sung in the build.
- Topics: euphoria, togetherness, nightlife, release, longing resolved on the dance floor.
- Write lyrics for the build and verses; the drop is often instrumental.

## Harmony
- Major: I - V - vi - IV; vi - IV - I - V; IV - I - V - vi.
- Minor: i - bVI - bIII - bVII; i - bVII - bVI - V; i - iv.
- Chords: triads, sus2/sus4, add9, 7ths on pads; minimal extensions.
- Modal habits: Aeolian (natural minor) and Dorian are common; major for uplifting styles.
- Cadences: loops that build with a pedal bass; the drop lands on the tonic.
- Keys: minor keys such as Am, Fm, Gm, Cm; a key shift up a whole step is a classic lift.
- Harmonic rhythm is slow: one chord per bar or per two bars.

## Rhythm
- Tempo: 118–150 BPM; house at 120–128 BPM, trance at 128–140 BPM, drum-and-bass at 170–175 BPM.
- Meter: 4/4.
- Feel: straight and quantized; sidechain "pump" creates groove.
- Groove: kick on every beat, offbeat open hi-hat on the "and" of each beat, clap on 2 and 4.
- Phrases: 8-bar and 16-bar blocks; builds of 8 bars with snare rolls.
- Sections change on multiples of 8 bars.

## Arrangement
- Core: drum machine, bass synth, lead synth, pads, risers, vocal sample or topline.
- Texture: layered, adding and removing elements every 8 bars; filters open on builds.
- Arc: intro (DJ-friendly), verse, build with riser and snare roll, drop, breakdown, second build, bigger drop, outro.
- Production: sidechain compression on pads, heavy low end, wide stereo synths, reverb tails, white-noise sweeps.
- Vocals: processed with pitch effects, vocoder, chops, doubling and filters.

## Adaptation notes
- Convert to 4/4 four-on-the-floor at 120-130 BPM; lay out an edm-build-drop structure.
- Reharmonize to a four-chord loop or two-chord pedal; keep chords as pads and the bass as root notes.
- Re-fit lyrics: cut to 3-6 syllable phrases, repeat the title in the build, free the drop for a synth hook.
- Pitfall: keeping the original dense verse lyrics, which clutter the groove.
- Pitfall: a drop with no contrast from the build.
- Pitfall: forgetting DJ-friendly intros and outros.
