# Genre profile: Hip-hop

## Identity
Rhythmic spoken delivery over a beat-driven loop, valuing flow, wordplay, and voice. The beat is the harmony; the rhyme is the melody.
Keep when adapting INTO hip-hop: a loop-based beat with a strong pocket, a rhythmic vocal, and verse-led storytelling.

## Lyric
- Diction: vernacular, dense with wordplay, slang, double meanings, and punchlines.
- POV: first-person; boast, testimony, or storytelling; occasional character voices.
- Lines: 10-16 syllables; bars of 16, with sixteenth-note subdivisions in flow.
- Rhyme: multisyllabic and internal rhyme, chains of assonance; couplets or longer rhyme runs over 2-4 bars.
- Imagery: streets, ambition, struggle, money, family, city life; vivid specifics.
- Form: verse-refrain with 16-bar verses and an 8-bar hook; VCVCBC with a short bridge also fits.
- Hook: a sung or chanted 4-8 bar refrain, repeated between verses.
- Topics: ambition, identity, community, social commentary, celebration, rivalry.
- Place the key line on a strong beat; the last word of each bar carries the rhyme.

## Harmony
- Harmony is static: a one-chord or two-chord loop under the whole verse.
- Minor: i - bVI; i - bVII; i - iv; i - bVI - bVII; i - i - iv - v.
- Major: I - IV; I - vi; vi - IV - I - V when a sample-like loop is needed.
- Colour: m7, m9, maj7, with dark pads or sampled-style textures.
- Bass line (808 sub) defines the harmony and moves by root notes.
- Cadences are avoided; loops repeat, with a drop-out for emphasis.
- Keys: minor keys such as Am, Cm, Fm, Gm; modulation is rare.

## Rhythm
- Tempo: 60–100 BPM felt in half-time (trap: 130–160 BPM with half-time snare).
- Meter: 4/4.
- Feel: straight or slightly swung sixteenths; triplet flow is common.
- Groove: kick on 1 and syncopated placements; snare or clap on 3 (half-time) or 2 and 4; hi-hat in eighths with rolls in sixteenth or triplet.
- Phrases: 4-bar and 2-bar units; 16-bar verses; 8-bar hooks.
- The vocal rides across the beat, with pauses at bar ends.

## Arrangement
- Core: drum machine or sampled drums, 808 or sub bass, sampled or synthesized loop, lead voice.
- Texture: minimal beat with a bold low end; vocals front and dry.
- Arc: intro tag, hook, verse 1, hook, verse 2, hook, bridge or breakdown, final hook, outro.
- Production: heavy sub bass, crisp snare, sidechain on the loop, vocal doubling and ad-libs panned wide.
- Vocals: rhythmic, close-miked, strong transients; ad-lib responses behind the lead.

## Adaptation notes
- Convert to a steady loop at 70-95 BPM (or 140-150 BPM with half-time); reduce harmony to two chords.
- Reharmonize toward minor loops with a sub bass; strip melodic chord motion.
- Re-fit lyrics: lengthen lines to 10-16 syllables, add internal and multisyllabic rhyme, move the hook to a sung refrain.
- Pitfall: keeping a busy chord progression that fights the vocal.
- Pitfall: lyrics without wordplay or specific detail.
- Pitfall: hi-hat patterns that crowd the vocal rhythm.
