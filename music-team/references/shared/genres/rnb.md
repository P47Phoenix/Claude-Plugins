# Genre profile: R&B

## Identity
Smooth, groove-centred vocal music: melismatic singing over warm chords and a deep pocket, centred on intimacy and feeling.
Keep when adapting INTO R&B: the vocal runs, the pocket, jazz-flavoured chords, and the emotional intimacy.

## Lyric
- Diction: conversational, sensual, emotionally candid; short phrases that leave room for vocal runs.
- POV: first-person to a lover; vulnerable, seductive, or reflective.
- Lines: 6-9 syllables; stretched across beats with melisma.
- Rhyme: loose; AABB or ABCB; repetition and ad-lib phrases matter more than clever rhyme.
- Imagery: night, touch, rooms, phones, light, perfume, distance; physical and domestic details.
- Form: VCVCBC with a pre-chorus; a vamp outro with ad-libs.
- Hook: a short melodic phrase repeated, often with a vocal run on the last repeat.
- Topics: desire, devotion, break-ups, trust, self-worth, late-night longing.
- Leave rests for breath and runs.

## Harmony
- Major: Imaj7 - vi7 - ii7 - V7sus; IV maj7 - iii7 - ii7 - Imaj9.
- Minor: i7 - iv7 - bVII7 - bIIImaj7; ii7 - V7 - i7.
- Chords: maj7, m7, m9, 11th, 13th; slash chords and sus voicings.
- Chromatic passing chords and tritone substitutions lend a jazzy flavour.
- Cadences: soft resolutions with sus4 to the tonic; deceptive moves to vi.
- Keys: Db, Eb, Ab, Bb for vocal warmth; a final-chorus lift is common.
- Modulation: a half-step lift at the last chorus.

## Rhythm
- Tempo: 60–110 BPM; slow jams at 60-80 BPM, mid-tempo at 85-105 BPM.
- Meter: 4/4; 12/8 for slow soul ballads.
- Feel: laid-back, swung sixteenths or shuffled hi-hats; the snare sits slightly behind.
- Groove: kick on 1 and "a" of 2, snare on 2 and 4, hi-hat in sixteenths with ghost notes; syncopated bass.
- Phrases: 4-bar and 8-bar; verses 8-16 bars.
- A groove pocket is more important than complexity.

## Arrangement
- Core: electric piano or keys, bass (electric or synth), drums or programmed beat, guitar licks, lead voice with stacked harmonies.
- Texture: smooth and layered, with space in the verse and lush backing in the chorus.
- Arc: sparse intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus with harmonies, bridge, vamp outro with ad-libs.
- Production: warm low end, soft compression, silky reverb on vocals, tight bass and drums.
- Vocals: melismatic runs, falsetto, breathy intimacy; stacked backing vocals in thirds and sixths.

## Adaptation notes
- Convert to a laid-back 4/4 at 70-100 BPM with swung sixteenths; add a syncopated bass line.
- Reharmonize with maj7/m7/m9 and sus chords; add chromatic passing chords.
- Re-fit lyrics: shorten phrases to leave gaps for runs, soften diction, repeat a short hook phrase.
- Pitfall: rigid, on-the-grid feel with no pocket.
- Pitfall: over-singing every note rather than leaving space.
- Pitfall: busy arrangement that masks the vocal.
