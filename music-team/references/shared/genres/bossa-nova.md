# Genre profile: Bossa nova

## Identity
A soft, intimate Brazilian-jazz hybrid: a syncopated nylon-string guitar pattern, whispered vocals, and lush chromatic harmony.
Keep when adapting INTO bossa nova: the guitar pattern, hushed delivery, and descending chromatic chords.

## Lyric
- Diction: gentle, understated, sensual; short words with open vowels that glide.
- POV: first-person, quiet confession or observation; addressed to a "you" at a distance.
- Lines: 5-8 syllables, flowing across the barline; conversational and unhurried.
- Rhyme: loose; AABB or ABAB with soft assonance; rhyme is not forced.
- Imagery: sea, sun, sand, rain, window light, a girl walking, small moments, saudade.
- Form: AABA (32 bars) or verse-refrain in 16-bar blocks; through-composed also appears.
- Hook: a recurring title phrase on a descending melodic figure.
- Topics: longing, quiet love, the beach, sea breezes, wistful reminiscence.
- Keep the language light; never push or belt emotion.

## Harmony
- Major: IMaj7 - ii7 - bIImaj7 - Imaj7; IMaj7 - VI7 - ii7 - V7.
- Minor: i m6 - iiø7 - V7(b9) - i; im7 - bVII7 - bVImaj7 - V7alt.
- Chords: maj7, m7, 9, 13, m6, with chromatic bass lines.
- Cadences: ii-V-I with tritone substitutions, often cut short by a chromatic descent.
- Modal habits: modal interchange to bII and bVII; parallel chromatic planing.
- Keys: D, G, C, F, A; guitar-friendly voicings.
- Modulation by a sudden ii-V into a new key during the bridge.

## Rhythm
- Tempo: 110–145 BPM, felt in two with a gentle sway.
- Meter: 4/4 (often written in 2/4).
- Feel: straight eighths with syncopation; no swing.
- Groove: bass on 1 and the "and" of 2 (dotted quarter + eighth), guitar thumb on bass while fingers play a syncopated cross-rhythm, rim click or brushed snare.
- Phrases: 8-bar sections; melody overlaps the barline.
- Keep the groove light; it should never feel pushed.

## Arrangement
- Core: nylon-string guitar, upright or electric bass, light drums (brushes or rim click), piano; optional flute or soft saxophone.
- Texture: transparent; each instrument stays soft and in its pocket.
- Arc: guitar intro, vocal verse, instrumental interlude, repeated vocal chorus, gentle fade or tag.
- Production: close-miked nylon guitar, soft room, very light compression, no big reverb tail.
- Vocals: breathy, nearly spoken, minimal vibrato, close to the microphone.

## Adaptation notes
- Convert to straight-eighth 4/4 at 115-140 BPM; place the syncopated guitar pattern under the vocal.
- Reharmonize with maj7, m7, and 9 chords; add chromatic descending bass and ii-V substitutions.
- Re-fit lyrics: soften to 5-8 syllables per line, drop hard consonants, let phrases flow across barlines.
- Pitfall: swinging the eighths and turning it into standard swing jazz.
- Pitfall: singing loudly or with heavy vibrato.
- Pitfall: busy drums that destroy the guitar pattern.
