# Genre: Pop

## Lyric conventions

- Present tense, first person addressing a second person is the default; the "you" is anchored by one object in Verse 1.
- Plain vocabulary: the hook uses words a listener knows at age ten. Rarity lives in one verse image per section, never in the chorus.
- The chorus states the hook in its first line or its last line; every chorus is word-identical unless `structure.md` gives a different meter spec.
- Pre-chorus lines build toward the hook with rising specificity and shorter words; the last pre-chorus line ends on a content word that is not in the hook.
- Repetition is a feature: a title phrase appears three to five times across the song. Repeat exact words, not near-synonyms.
- Verses are short on syllables per line (7 to 9) so the chorus can feel wider.

## Rhyme density

High. Choruses use full rhyme on every lettered line (`AABB` or `ABAB`); slant is tolerated in verses, not in the chorus. Internal rhyme inside a chorus line is welcome once. Feminine endings (`-ing`, `-er`) are rare at chorus line ends because the final beat lands `u`.

## Taboo moves

- Explaining the feeling the chorus already delivers ("and that's why I feel...").
- Stacking abstractions: heart, soul, dreams, forever, fire. One per song at most; none in the hook.
- Similes at line end ("like a ...") are the cliché list's richest vein.
- Verse detail that names a real brand, place, or person; `must_avoid[]` in pop briefs usually names "specific city names".
- A bridge that is a third verse; the pop bridge changes tense or vantage in its first line.
- Line-final function words; every lettered line ends on a noun or verb.

## Typical section lengths

| Section | Lines | Syllables per line |
|---------|-------|--------------------|
| Verse | 4 | 7-9 |
| Pre-Chorus | 2 | 8-10 |
| Chorus | 4 | 7-9, hook line shortest |
| Bridge | 4 | 8-9, one line of 9-10 allowed |

`hook_max_syllables`: no override (default 12). Pop hooks that land sit at 6 to 9.

## Three original example lines

```text
Nobody knows my name here yet            (hook: 8 syllables, withheld last word)
I learn the bus routes upside down       (verse: one action, one object)
The windows glow but none of them say stay   (pre-chorus: evidence turning into a choice)
```
