# Genre: Country

## Common progressions (Roman numerals, one cell per bar)

- Verse: `| I | I | IV | I |` then `| I | V | V | I |` (the three-chord frame), or `| I | IV | I | V |`
- Pre-chorus (when used): `| ii | IV | V | V |`, `| IV | IV | V | V |`
- Chorus: `| I | IV | I | V |` then `| I | IV | V | I |`; or `| IV | I | V | I |` twice
- Bridge: `| vi | IV | I | V |`, or the modal lean `| bVII | IV | I | I |`
- Two-chord stretches (`| I | I |`, `| V | V |`) are idiomatic; `%` repeats are normal in a chart.

## Tempo bands

| Mood | bpm |
|------|-----|
| ballad, waltz | 66-84 (waltz in `3/4`) |
| story, walking (default) | 92-110 |
| train beat, upbeat | 112-128 |
| barn dance | 128-140 |

`4/4` default; `3/4` for a waltz is common and welcome; `6/8` for a slow rolling ballad.

## Feel

Backbeat on 2 and 4 with a walking or two-beat bass (root on 1, fifth on 3); the train beat is straight eighths on the snare; shuffle (swung eighths) for a honky-tonk brief. Verses are 8 or 16 bars; the chorus 8; bridges short (4 to 8) or absent; a 4-bar instrumental turnaround after the second chorus is expected.

## Typical keys

Major: `G`, `D`, `A`, `C`, `E`; minor is rare and signals a dark brief (`E minor`, `A minor`). Keep to one or two sharps; open-string keys for guitar.

## Melodic idioms

- Pentatonic-leaning verses (degrees `1 2 3 5 6`); the `4` and `7` arrive in the chorus.
- Story verses sit on repeated pitches with a drop of a third at line ends; the chorus rises a fourth or fifth and holds the hook's last word on a long note (`_` melisma over two notes at most).
- The hook ends on `1` or `3`; a `5` ending leaves the title open, used once in the bridge.
- Grace-note slides are an arranging matter; write the plain note.
- Range stays narrow (an octave or less across the whole song) so a non-trained voice carries it.

## Threshold overrides

`diatonic_min_ratio`: no override (default 0.6). Country charts that pass sit at 0.9 or above; the one borrowed chord is `bVII` or `iv`, once.
