## Validation Rules

When validating a decklist:

1. **Exact name match**: Compare card names case-insensitively against this list.
2. **Commander check**: A banned card cannot be used as commander OR in the 99.
3. **No exceptions**: There are no "banned as commander only" cards in the current list. All bans are universal.

## Cards NOT Banned (Common Misconceptions)

These cards are frequently assumed to be banned but are currently legal in Commander:

- Sol Ring (legal)
- Thassa's Oracle (legal)
- Demonic Tutor (legal)
- Cyclonic Rift (legal)
- Rhystic Study (legal)
- Smothering Tithe (legal)
- Expropriate (legal)
- Thoracle combo pieces individually (legal -- the combo itself is not banned, only individual cards can be banned)
