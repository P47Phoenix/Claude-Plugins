# Investor Pitch

**Detection keywords**: "investor pitch", "fundraising deck", "pitch to investors"

**Pipeline auto-detection**: UAT stage with `audience: investor` config.

**Required artifacts**: Idea brief or PRD, traction/metrics data.
**Enhancing artifacts**: Competitive analysis, financial projections, team bios.

**Narrative arc**: Problem-Solution-Result with market lens — TAM, traction, ask. See `references/narrative-patterns.md`.

**Audience defaults**: Investor — market opportunity, growth, competitive advantage; minimize technical depth.
