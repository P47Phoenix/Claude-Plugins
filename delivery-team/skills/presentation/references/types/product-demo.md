# Product Demo

**Detection keywords**: "product demo", "feature demo", "show what we built", "demo for publisher"

**Pipeline auto-detection**: Development after feature completion.

**Required artifacts**: At least 1 feature artifact (FKC, implementation doc, or UAT report).
**Enhancing artifacts**: Screenshots, user feedback, metrics.

**Narrative arc**: Before-After — what existed, what's new, the transformation. See `references/narrative-patterns.md`.

**GAME_DEV variant**: When `project.type: GAME_DEV` and type is Product Demo, use "publisher milestone" vocabulary and structure the demo around gameplay mechanics rather than feature lists. Replace "feature demo" with "milestone demo" in slide titles.

**Audience defaults**: Customer/client-facing or executive — frame in user workflow terms.
