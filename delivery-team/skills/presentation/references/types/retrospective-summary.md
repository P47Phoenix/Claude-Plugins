# Retrospective Summary

**Detection keywords**: "retro summary", "retrospective presentation", "what we learned"

**Pipeline auto-detection**: Post-retrospective.

**Required artifacts**: Retrospective notes or action items.
**Enhancing artifacts**: Velocity trends, defect data, previous retro actions.

**Narrative arc**: Tension-Resolution — challenges encountered, themes identified, action items committed. See `references/narrative-patterns.md`.

## Sensitivity and Disclaimer

**Disclaimer** (always displayed for Retrospective Summary, regardless of audience):

> "This presentation summarizes team retrospective themes. Individual feedback has been anonymized and generalized."

**Sensitivity filter** (audience-dependent):
- **"executive" or "client-facing" audience**: Apply sensitivity filter — generalize individual feedback to team patterns, omit names from specific feedback, frame challenges as process improvements not personnel issues. See `references/narrative-patterns.md` for detailed rules.
- **"technical" or "casual" audience**: Sensitivity filter does NOT apply — full detail from retrospective notes is preserved (team-internal audiences).
