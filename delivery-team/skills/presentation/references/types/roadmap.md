# Roadmap

**Detection keywords**: "roadmap", "quarterly plan", "what's coming next"

**Pipeline auto-detection**: Plan after roadmap/quarterly planning.

**Required artifacts**: Sprint plan or backlog, pipeline state.
**Enhancing artifacts**: Architecture roadmap, risk register, resource allocation.

**Narrative arc**: Now/Next/Later — locked positional groups (the three time horizons do not reorder). See `references/narrative-patterns.md`.

**Audience defaults**: Executive or stakeholder; framing varies by audience.

**Position-locked**: Now/Next/Later groups are framework-locked positions — narrative tension and emphasis passes do not move slides across these groups.
