## Design Decisions

1. **5 review categories for layout** (vs. 7 for schematic review): Layout review has different domain concerns than schematic review. The 5 categories (placement, routing, stackup, DRC, manufacturing readiness) cover the PCB layout domain comprehensively without artificially inflating category count.

2. **Output templates included in SKILL.md**: Layout Review Report and Stackup Specification templates are embedded directly in SKILL.md (Level 2 context) rather than in reference files, because they are always needed when the skill is invoked. Reference files contain domain knowledge loaded on demand.

3. **DRC severity classification**: Four-tier severity (critical/major/minor/info) with clear gate-blocking rules. Critical and major block the gate; minor and info are documented but do not block. This matches the architecture's gate framework.
