---

## TL;DR

The PRD is well-cited and the model-delta research is accurate (4/4 load-bearing claims verified against source). But two material issues sit inside it: (a) the "time-sensitive retirement" urgency for MID-03 is **overstated** because neither plugin that hosts those IDs imports the Anthropic SDK — they're registry metadata, not API calls, and (b) the Architect is handed a **keystone gap** on `delivery-team/skills/product-delivery/SKILL.md` (685 lines, never inventoried line-by-line) even though the PRD's own rubric flags files this size as keystone-adjacent. Revisions recommended before Architect entry.

---

## Confidence Rating

**Confidence: 3 / 5 — Notable gaps, revisions recommended before Architect.**

Justification: Research is solid and the keystone concentration claim holds, but the PRD takes two under-validated shortcuts (MID-03 urgency, product-delivery/SKILL.md depth) that would let the Architect build a roadmap sequenced around a non-urgency and under-sized on a keystone-adjacent file. Neither is fatal; both are cheap to fix at the PO stage and expensive to fix after the Architect writes a roadmap on top.

## Closing Adversarial Summary — Top 3 Concerns in Priority Order

1. **C-01 / C-07 — "Urgency" frame on MID-03 retirement is unsupported.** The PRD carries a retirement-date narrative (R-05 High impact, AC-01.2 "time-sensitive", Open Question 2 "ship REQ-01 first standalone") predicated on an assumption that the model IDs are API call targets. They aren't — zero SDK imports in the two affected plugins. The PRD even flags this as UV-01 and UV-02 but doesn't resolve it before handing to Architect. Fix: resolve UV-01/UV-02 in §3.1 (one SDK-import grep), downgrade R-05, and strip the time-sensitive flag. Without this fix the Architect will build urgency-driven sequencing on a non-urgency. **Blast-radius: Medium. Fix cost: 5 minutes.**

2. **C-02 — `product-delivery/SKILL.md` (685 LOC) is silently under-scoped for F-25 / F-26 prose review.** It sits in the same size band as the keystone files the PRD *does* flag, it is the single largest PO/SM/DA instruction surface in the repo, and the PRD's grep-based inventory (§3.3) used API-shape patterns that cannot detect prose-level under-specification (the very thing F-25 warns about). Fix: broaden REQ-02's keystone prose-read scope to all SKILL.md files >650 LOC. **Blast-radius: High. Fix cost: one line in REQ-02 plus one in §3.7.**

3. **C-03 — `research-agent/SKILL.md` is not flagged keystone despite the system-prompt labelling it keystone and F-07 applying to it directly.** The PRD's grep-only inventory missed this because research-agent carries no 4.6-era *API patterns* — but F-07's "fewer tool calls, more reasoning" is a *behavioural* regression that silently breaks research quality and will never be caught by a grep. No REQ covers research-agent dogfood. Fix: add a SHOULD REQ for research-agent dogfood probe with tool-use count assertion, or amend REQ-03 to cover research-agent alongside delivery-flow. **Blast-radius: High. Fix cost: one REQ plus one metric (M-08).**

**Recommendation to the pipeline**: Confidence 3 → NOT_DONE → send back to the PO for targeted revision on C-01, C-02, C-03. The other findings (C-04 through C-11) are useful context for the Architect but do not require PO revision. The research in §2 is sound and can ship unchanged.
