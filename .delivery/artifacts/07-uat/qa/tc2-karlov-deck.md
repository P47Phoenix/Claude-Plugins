## Game Plan

Karlov grows rapidly from incremental lifegain triggers (Soul Warden, extort, lifelink creatures), becoming a removal engine and lethal threat. The deck drains opponents through Vito / Sanguine Bond / Cliffhaven Vampire converting each lifegain event into damage, while payoff cards like Crested Sunmare and Felidar Sovereign provide alternative win conditions. The low mana curve fuels extort triggers and keeps Karlov's counters flowing.

## Strategy Deep-Dive

### Core Engine
Karlov triggers on **any** lifegain event, not just one per turn. This means incremental 1-life triggers from Soul Warden, Authority of the Consuls, and extort generate disproportionate value — each trigger adds 2 counters. With 3-4 lifegain sources active, Karlov grows by 6-8 counters per turn cycle.

### Drain Layer
Vito, Sanguine Bond, Cliffhaven Vampire, Marauding Blight-Priest, and Epicure of Blood convert each lifegain event into opponent life loss. With multiple drain pieces active, a single Soul Warden trigger from an opponent's creature ETB deals 2-5 damage across the table while growing Karlov.

### Win Conditions (5 paths)
1. **Commander damage**: Karlov grows to lethal (21 combat) within 2-3 turn cycles
2. **Drain accumulation**: Vito/Sanguine Bond + lifegain triggers drain opponents incrementally
3. **Gray Merchant of Asphodel**: Devotion burst drain (typically 8-15 per opponent)
4. **Felidar Sovereign**: Win at 40+ life on upkeep
5. **Test of Endurance**: Win at 50+ life on upkeep

### Power Level Assessment
Rated 6 (focused casual): The deck has a clear, resilient strategy with redundant pieces but no infinite combos, no fast mana beyond Sol Ring, and no oppressive stax elements. Games close in turns 6-10 typically. No land destruction included per user restriction.

### Cards Cut for $10 Per-Card Cap
The following high-synergy cards were excluded to comply with the $10 cap:
- Exquisite Blood ($28.62) — would have enabled infinite combo with Sanguine Bond
- Archangel of Thune ($60.29) — premium lifegain payoff
- Heliod, Sun-Crowned ($23.53) — lifegain counter distribution
- Aetherflux Reservoir ($21.05) — 50 life = 50 damage win condition
- Crypt Ghast ($20.90) — mana doubler with extort
- Serra Ascendant ($32.64) — 1-mana 6/6 flyer
- Boon Reflection ($15.21) — double lifegain
- Alhammarret's Archive ($11.95) — double lifegain + draw
- Beacon of Immortality ($13.91) — double life total
- Angel of Destiny ($10.24) — alternative win condition
- Witch's Clinic ($12.14) — commander lifelink land

These cuts were offset by including budget-friendly redundant pieces (Epicure of Blood, Marauding Blight-Priest, Celestial Unicorn, etc.) that maintain the deck's synergy density at 3.4 average interactions.
